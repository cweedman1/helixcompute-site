Three Ways to Do More With Less

Compute Less.

Process the work affected by change instead of recomputing everything that remains valid.

Move Less.

When the destination already has most of the state, move what changed instead of another complete copy when appropriate.

Store Less.

Reconstruct historical states without storing every generation in full when appropriate.

How It Works

    
Governed Reconstruction


    

      Helix keeps enough information to verify a change against known state before accepting it. When the change is sufficient, Helix reconstructs the exact next state. When it isn't, Helix can use complete state instead.
    


    

      

        
Keep What Is Known.

        
Start from known-good state.

      


      

        
Verify the Change.

        
Confirm the change belongs to the state being advanced.

      


      

        
Reconstruct or Use Complete State.

        
Reconstruct when appropriate. Fall back when it is not.

Where It Applies


    

      Helix is most useful when a relatively small change affects a much larger body of retained data.
    


    
    
      
  • Data pipelines where most existing results remain valid
    • 
      
  • Data delivery where the destination already has prior state
    • 
      
  • Historical systems that need exact earlier generations on demand
